-------------------------------------------------
THE SIMPLE INFO
-------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS
A cashflow calculator for investors running 1-20 units.

WHAT IT DOES
Shows the gap between what people think they make and what they
actually net. Demo portfolio: $2,054 looks like profit. Real
number is $573. The $1,481 difference is reserves most
spreadsheets never subtract.

WHAT IT CALCULATES
  True net cashflow    what actually lands in the account
  NOI                  what a lender underwrites
  Cap rate             yield before financing
  Cash-on-cash         return on cash actually invested
  DSCR                 badged BANKABLE / THIN / UNDERWATER
  Expense ratio        operational efficiency
  Rent-to-price        the 1% rule, computed
  Net per unit         compares a duplex to a triplex

FEATURES
  - Click any property for a full line-by-line breakdown
  - Two debt modes: loan terms (computes P&I live) or known payment
  - Saves in the browser automatically
  - Export CSV, print clean PDF
  - Works offline once loaded

PRIVACY
Nothing is uploaded. No account, no tracking, no database.
Everything saves in the user's own browser only.
