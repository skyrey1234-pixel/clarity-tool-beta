WHAT IT IS

A cashflow calculator that doesn't lie to you.

Built for investors running 1–20 units. Enter a property, get the number a lender would actually underwrite — plus the one that hits your account.

01
It counts what you forget

Vacancy, maintenance, CapEx and management are real costs. Most spreadsheets skip them. This one doesn't.

02
It speaks lender

NOI, cap rate, cash-on-cash and DSCR — computed the way an underwriter computes them.

03
It shows its work

Click any property for a full line-by-line breakdown. Every number is traceable. Nothing is hidden.

WHAT YOU GET

Eight metrics, instantly.

Per property and blended across your whole portfolio. Recalculates the moment anything changes.

  • True net cashflow — what actually lands
  • NOI — what lenders underwrite
  • Cap rate — yield before financing
  • Cash-on-cash — return on your capital
  • DSCR — badged bankable / thin / underwater
  • Expense ratio — operational efficiency
  • Rent-to-price — the 1% rule, computed
  • Net per unit — compare any two deals
